How To Play Binance Crypto Wodl?

If you are interested in playing Binance Crypto WODL, just follow these simple instructions:

  • Make your Binance account first. Make sure you complete your KYC.
  • Now, just log in to your newly created account and then click on More.
  • Now scroll down and click on the "Word of the Day" option.
  • You have to click on the correct crypto/blockchain-related word to qualify for a game.

After logging into the game, you'll notice that the mechanics are just like the popular Wordle game. You need to fill in the boxes with the letters that came to mind first. Each of the character tiles will then be highlighted with a different color, indicating whether they are part of the final word or if they are positioned in the incorrect box.

Here are the WODL colors:

  • Green
  • This letter is in the right position. Good job!
  • Yellow
  • The letter is part of the word, but it is not in the right position. Try scrambling the letters around; you might find a clue!
  • Black
  • That's a letter, not part of the word.

You can only play and guess two words for a day, and there'll be a new answer delivered within 24 hours. Remember, you only have six tries to guess the correct word. Lose once, and you'll only have one last chance to answer again. If you lost those two chances, you'll have to try the next day.

What Is the Binance Crypto WODL Game?

To celebrate the fifth anniversary of the Binance crypto exchange, Binance created the Crypto WODL game and introduced it to the world.

The company invented this game to attract more users to the Binance world and gain more consumer support. Players try to guess the correct blockchain or cryptocurrency-related term to unlock free crypto rewards.
