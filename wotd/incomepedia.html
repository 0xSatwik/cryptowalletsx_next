How to Access the Word of the Day on Binance

Follow these steps to start solving the WOTD puzzle on Binance:

  1. Open the Binance app.
  2. Tap the Binance icon at the top-left corner of the screen.
  3. Click on “More Services” and find WOTD under the “Gift & Campaign” section.
  4. Click on it, read the hint, and start guessing the word. You can check your winnings in Reward Hub once the activity is ended.

You can unlock a new word by sharing the WOTD with your friends!

How to Play Binance Word of the Day

You get 6 attempts each day to guess the correct Word of the Day (WOTD). After each guess, the letter tiles change colors to give you feedback—just like in Wordle. This color-coding is key to solving the puzzle strategically:

  • 🟩 Green Tile: The letter is correct and in the correct position.
    Example: If you guessed the word SMART and the letter S turns green, it means S is the first letter of the answer. Lock it in and keep it there in future guesses.
  • 🟧 Orange Tile: The letter is in the word but in the wrong position.
    Example: If M turns orange, the word contains M, but it’s not where you placed it. Try placing it in different spots in your next guess.
  • ⬜ Grey Tile: The letter is not in the word at all.
    Example: If D, R, and U all turn grey, eliminate them from future guesses. Including them again wastes precious attempts.

  • The letter M is in the word but not in the correct position (Orange tile).
  • The letters D, R, and U are not in the word (Grey tiles).

Use this color feedback wisely after each try to narrow down possibilities. Over time, you’ll spot patterns and build the correct word logically. Smart elimination and repositioning are the key to solving the puzzle within six guesses!Tools

Tips to Solve Binance WOTD (Word of the Day)

  • Bookmark this page for daily WOTD answers
    This is the simplest and most reliable method to earn Binance Points consistently. Our daily updates ensure you don’t miss a correct guess and help you maintain your winning streak.
  • Experiment with different letters
    Start by trying out 4–5 different words using a variety of common letters. Pay close attention to the color feedback after each guess:
    • 🟩 Green: The letter is correct and in the right position.
    • 🟧 Orange: The letter is part of the word but in a different spot.
    • ⬜ Grey: The letter isn’t in the word at all.
      Based on this, eliminate grey letters from future attempts, move orange letters around, and keep green letters locked in their positions. This process of elimination sharpens your accuracy with each guess.
  • Ask an AI agent for help
    If you’ve discovered part of the word (e.g., it’s a 5-letter word ending in “PH”), ask an AI like ChatGPT for word suggestions matching that pattern. This can dramatically narrow your options and save your guess limit.
  • Use a word-finder tool
    Leverage online tools like word pattern solvers or “Wordle helper” websites. You can enter known letters and their positions (or exclusions), and these tools will return a list of valid word possibilities. It’s a great shortcut when you’re stuck on your last 1–2 guesses.
  • Pay attention to the provided hints
    Binance usually shares a hint with each WOTD puzzle—read it carefully! These clues often relate to current crypto topics, trending tokens, or blockchain concepts. For example, if the hint says “used for crypto transactions,” the word might be TOKEN, CHAIN, or WALLET. Don’t skip this—sometimes, the hint gives away more than you expect.

These tips can make a big difference in solving the WOTD quickly and accurately. With only six attempts available each day, a mix of smart guessing, tool usage, and context awareness can ensure you don’t miss out on those valuable Binance points.

These points can be redeemed in the Rewards Shop for various benefits, such as token vouchers, trading rebate vouchers, and other exclusive items.
